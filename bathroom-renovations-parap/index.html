Renovating a bathroom in Parap

Parap is an inner Darwin suburb with older tropical houses, units and the well-known Saturday markets.

Bathrooms in older Parap homes are often small and were built before current waterproofing standards. Asbestos-containing sheeting was common in older wet areas and must be removed safely.

What to mention on your quote request

Tell us the suburb (Parap), which bathroom (main, ensuite or powder room), what you'd like to change and any timing needs. We pass that to one local bathroom renovator who services Parap, and they contact you directly. The request is free, and there's no obligation to go ahead.

Rules and approvals

Parap is in the City of Darwin area. In the Northern Territory, prescribed residential building work must be done by a registered building contractor under a written contract, with a fidelity fund certificate before a permit and before any payment, and the standard deposit cap is 5%.
