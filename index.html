Renovating a bathroom in Darwin

Darwin's inner and northern suburbs have a wide mix of bathrooms to renovate: compact wet areas in older tropical houses and units in Parap, Fannie Bay, Stuart Park and The Gardens, and later bathrooms in Nightcliff, Rapid Creek, Millner and Coconut Grove. High humidity makes ventilation and sound waterproofing especially important, and older wet areas can hide failed membranes.

Units may need body corporate approval. In the Northern Territory, plumbing and electrical work must be done by licensed practitioners, and a building permit is needed for some structural changes but not for every like-for-like renovation. Ask the renovator what approvals apply to your job, and keep waterproofing and trade certificates at handover.

Does the waterproofer need a licence?

Waterproofing must meet the National Construction Code and AS 3740. Ask who will do the work, whether they are licensed for the relevant trade, and for a certificate when it's done. See our waterproofing guide.

What licence should the renovator have?

In the Northern Territory, prescribed residential building work must be done by a registered building contractor under a written contract, with a fidelity fund certificate before a permit and before any payment, and the standard deposit cap is 5%.
